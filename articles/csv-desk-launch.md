# CSV Desk batch edition is available

Disclosure: we made CSV Desk.

I built CSV Desk for recurring CSV handoffs: inspect duplicate headers and quoted multiline notes, choose your cleanup, then download separate output files with original-byte backups and a JSON change report.

The single-file demo is free and works without an upload: https://ntoledo319.github.io/many-routes-csv-desk-demo/. The offline batch edition is $29: https://payhip.com/b/WNTKh.

Tested in Chrome on Linux. Ten files max, 5 MiB each; no XLSX or fuzzy matching. IDs stay strings in the tool, but you still need Text import settings in your spreadsheet. Examples use fictional data.

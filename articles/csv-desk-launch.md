# CSV Desk batch edition is available

Disclosure: we made CSV Desk.

CSV Desk cleans up to ten CSV files at a time in your browser, offline. It flags repeated headers, duplicate records and formula-like cells, keeps quoted multiline notes together, and downloads a ZIP with a cleaned copy of each file, byte-for-byte backups and a JSON change report.

The single-file demo is free and works without an upload: https://ntoledo319.github.io/many-routes-csv-desk-demo/. The offline batch edition is $29: https://payhip.com/b/WNTKh.

Tested in Chrome on Linux. Ten files max, 5 MiB each; no XLSX or fuzzy matching. IDs stay strings in the tool, but you still need Text import settings in your spreadsheet. Examples use fictional data. Made with AI assistance: the code, docs and images were produced with AI tools, then tested as described.
